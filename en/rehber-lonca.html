    Guild

    Join or found a guild, the guild boss and the guild shop.

    GuildGuild ShopFounding and growing a guild

    Guild

    • Fight a shared boss together with your guild.
    • Every attack earns guild tokens.
    • The player who lands the killing blow earns extra tokens.

    Guild Shop

    • Spend guild tokens here. They are earned from the guild boss and the weekly ranking.
    • Only guild tokens work here; other tokens cannot be used.
    • If your bag is full, the item waits and arrives when there is room.

    Founding and growing a guild

    • You can found a guild from level 10 for 200 diamonds, or JOIN a guild with a free spot (some ask you to APPLY).
    • A level 1 guild has room for 25 members, and each guild level adds one more spot.
    • Members level the guild up together by DONATING gold and materials. Donations cannot be taken back.
    • Before each level up the guild must beat its GUILD LEVEL BOSS once. Each member can attack it once a day.
    • The week's first kill gives Guild Boxes to the top damage dealers; the boxes can hold charms.
    • Guild tokens come from boss attacks, the killing blow and the weekly ranking, and stay yours if you leave. Spend them in the GUILD SHOP; bought items are bound to you.
    • GUILD BLESSING: once a week, from guild level 2, it raises adventure experience for a day; the higher the guild level, the bigger the bonus.
