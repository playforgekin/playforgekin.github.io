    Dismantle

    What it is

    Break gear you do not need into Upgrade Stones of its tier and essence of its rarity.

    How to get there

    1. INVENTORY.
    2. Tap the item, then ••• menu: Salvage.
    3. For many items: BULK SALVAGE in the bag's action row.

    What you get

    • Upgrade Stones of the item's tier: a higher '+' gives more.
    • Essence of the item's rarity: Normal gear gives Normal Essence, Rare gives Rare Essence and so on. A higher tier gives more.
    • The confirmation shows MATERIALS YOU GET before anything is broken.

    How to do it

    • One item: tap it, then ••• and Salvage (stones + essence).
    • Many items: BULK SALVAGE in the bag, switch SALVAGE: ON, tap the items, then BREAK and CONFIRM SALVAGE.

    Watch out

    • Only gear, accessories and tools can be dismantled. Take an item off before dismantling it.
    • Items listed on the market and starter items cannot be dismantled.
    • A bound item can be dismantled, but what comes out is bound too.
    • If your bag is full, the materials wait and arrive when there is room.
    • Dismantling cannot be undone.
