    Tools

    What it is

    Gathering tools (pickaxe, rod, sickle) and workshop tools speed up their profession. Equip them in the tool slot.

    How to get there

    1. INVENTORY.
    2. Tap the TOOL slot: TOOL CABINET.

    Gathering tools

    • Pickaxe for Mining, Rod for Fishing, Sickle for Herbalism. They make gathering faster.
    • They need a PROFESSION level, not a character level. They wear while gathering and can be repaired.
    • They drop from creatures in the hunting grounds and are sold on the market.

    Workshop tools

    • One tool per processing or crafting profession. It shortens the time of its own profession only.
    • It only counts when your profession level is high enough. Workshop tools never wear.
    • They can get '+' upgrades, quality upgrades and tier transfer, and can be dismantled; no rerolls or runes.
    • They come from Expedition and Survival bosses and from the market.

    Tool Cabinet

    • One tool per profession. Filter by RARITY, PROFESSION and TIER or sort by speed, then EQUIP.
    • Tools can be sold on the market and traded.
