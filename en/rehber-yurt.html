    Hometown

    The buildings of your Hometown and what upgrading them does.

    Hometown

    • Each Hometown building belongs to some professions. Tap a building to see it.
    • Each level makes this building's professions craft faster.
    • Reductions add up and come off the base time.
    • Upgrading needs gold, materials and one of the building's professions at a certain level.

    Step by step

    1. PROCESSING holds Smelting, Alchemy and Cooking. CRAFTING HALL holds Blacksmithing, Armorsmith, Jewelcrafting and Weaponsmith.
    2. SHIPYARD builds your ship; the EXPEDITION GATE leads to Adventure. The Town Hall is still under construction.
    3. Open a building and use UPGRADE BUILDING. Upgrading needs gold, materials and sometimes one of the building's professions at a certain level. It cannot be undone.
