    Expeditions

    Walk the map, beat the wardens and the boss, find crafting tools.

    ExpeditionsExpedition areasAuto runs

    Expeditions

    • Move with the joystick or tap to walk. Walk into a monster to fight.
    • Expeditions are where CRAFTING TOOLS drop, by chance. Every mode is open.
    • Higher modes: tougher mobs, a better tool chance. Tool tier comes from the area.
    • Daily attempts are shared by all areas, auto runs included. Leaving ends the run and uses its attempt, but rewards already won are kept.
    • An auto run needs your power to reach the mode's recommended power.
    • Equip the tools you find in the TOOL CABINET to make crafting faster.

    Step by step

    1. Open the Battle camp and tap the EXPEDITION gate. The map of expedition areas opens.
    2. Pick an area, then a mode (Normal, Rare, Epic, Legendary). The panel shows the recommended power next to yours and the possible drops.
    3. Tap ENTER. Move with the joystick or tap to walk; the minimap fills in as you explore.
    4. Walk into a monster to fight it. Defeat both wardens to open the boss gate, then challenge the boss.
    5. Beating the boss clears the expedition. If time runs out, the expedition is lost.
    6. LEAVE ends the run and uses its entry, but rewards already won are kept. An unfinished run can be continued from the map.
    7. Loot waits in your reward pool; pet items go straight to the pet bag.

    Expedition areas

    • Each area's boss can drop crafting tools of that area's tier. Higher modes keep the same map but bring tougher monsters and a better tool chance.
    • A mode opens when you clear the one before it; an area opens when you clear the area before it.
    • Expeditions give experience and items, not gold.

    Auto runs

    • After you clear a mode yourself once, SEND sends your hero on an auto run. It uses one of today's entries, gives items only, and cannot be called back.
