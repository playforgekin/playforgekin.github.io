    Market and trade

    Sell, buy, trade with players, the bank and the help chest.

    MarketTradeBankExchangeHelp ChestGood to know

    Market

    • SELL: list your item; when it sells the gold lands in your account.
    • BUY ORDER: leave a price for an item; when someone sells, it comes to you.
    • You do NOT see your own listing in the list — cancel it from MY ORDERS.
    • MY ORDERS also shows pending deliveries; 'Collect item' puts them in your bag.
    • Bound items cannot be listed.
    • MARKET STALL: use a Market Stall Permit from your bag to open your stall in the Square. Other players can see your stall and its listings there.
    • A permit lasts for its own time. When it ends the stall closes, but your listings stay on the market (MY ORDERS).

    Step by step

    1. To sell: open an item and tap SELL, set the unit price and amount, and create the sell order.
    2. To buy: tap Buy on a listing; the screen shows the tax and the total before you pay.
    3. A buy order reserves your gold until someone sells to it.
    4. MY ORDERS: cancel a listing to get the item back, withdraw a buy order to get the gold back, and COLLECT your sale earnings.

    Trade

    • Item and gold exchange between two players.
    • Items placed on the table CANNOT be taken back — cancel the trade instead.
    • Any change to the offer RESETS both confirmations (anti-scam).
    • Quality, '+' level and wear carry over EXACTLY to the other side.
    • Bound items cannot be traded.

    Step by step

    1. Tap INVITE on a friend's card in your friends list. Both players must be online.
    2. Tap items in your bag and PUT ON THE TABLE; you can add gold too.
    3. Both players tap CONFIRM. Any change resets both confirmations.
    4. CANCEL TRADE sends everything back to its owner. Closing the game cancels the trade.

    Bank

    • Shared storage: park items here to free up bag space.
    • Items in the bank cannot be listed — move them to your bag first.
    • You can buy more slots with diamonds.

    Exchange

    • Spend exchange tokens here. Tokens come from the memory game and weekly badges.
    • Trade tokens for an upgrade stone that fits your level, Rare Essence or Normal Essence.
    • What you take waits in Held items.

    Help Chest

    • Donate weapons, armor and jewelry you no longer need. They go to new players.
    • Gone from your bag for good. No gold back - it's a gift.
    • Only low-tier items can be donated; bound items and items on the market cannot.
    • Donations earn Helpful points for the weekly badges. Points per day are limited.
    • New players below a level limit pick gifts from the chest every week.
    • Picked item arrives BOUND (can't be traded or sold)

    Good to know

    • When you buy, you pay the market tax on top; the seller gets the full unit price.
    • A Market Stall Permit opens your stall in the Square for a while: pick up to 3 of your listings to show there.
    • Bound items cannot be listed or traded.
