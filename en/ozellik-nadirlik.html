    Rarity

    What it is

    Every piece of gear has a rarity: Normal (grey), Rare (blue), Epic (purple) or Legendary (orange). Higher rarity means stronger stats, more bonus stat lines and more rune slots.

    How to get there

    1. Tap any item: the frame colour and the info panel show its rarity.

    Where it comes from

    • Creatures in the hunting grounds drop Normal gear.
    • Dungeons drop Rare gear, and the dungeon lord can drop Epic weapons.
    • Materials have no rarity.

    Raising rarity

    • Upgrade Quality raises an item one rarity step, up to Legendary.
    • Tier transfer moves a Legendary item to the next tier and keeps it Legendary.

    Watch out

    • Rarity and Quality are different things. Quality is a value in the item info, set when the item drops; only a reroll changes it.
    • Dismantling gives essence of the item's rarity.
