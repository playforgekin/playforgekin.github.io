    Improving gear

    '+' upgrades, quality, rerolls, runes and tier transfer.

    UpgradeQuality UpgradeReroll and RunesTier TransferQuality upgrade materials

    Upgrade

    • Adds '+' to an item. Every step raises its stats.
    • You need upgrade STONES; the cost rises with item tier and '+' level.
    • Failed attempts RAISE the next chance (pity).
    • Past a certain '+' level, failure BREAKS the item.
    • A PROTECTION STONE raises the chance and, at high '+' levels, keeps the item from breaking. One is spent on each attempt.

    Quality Upgrade

    • Raises an item one quality tier: Normal -> Rare -> Epic -> Legendary.
    • You need ESSENCE of the item's quality + the profession materials of its tier.
    • Epic and Legendary also need a DUNGEON CORE (drops in dungeons).
    • You do not have to craft the materials yourself — buy them on the market.
    • Higher quality gives better stats and more RUNE SLOTS.
    • The upgrade lowers your '+' level a little — that is a deliberate cost.

    Reroll and Runes

    • Both spend ESSENCE and gold. Essence comes from dismantling items.
    • REROLL: quality and all bonus stats are rolled again. Outcome unknown, cheap.
    • RUNE: YOU pick the stat. Its value is rolled once, then stays in the slot. Costs more.
    • Rerolling does NOT touch runes — an imprinted rune survives.
    • Rune slots depend on QUALITY: Normal has none, higher tiers open more.
    • A REROLL always takes Normal Essence; a RUNE always takes Legendary Essence. Lower essence can be merged upward.

    Tier Transfer

    • Moves a high '+' Legendary item up one tier, into a shell item of the next tier (any rarity).
    • The old item is used up and the '+' level drops a little. The shell's own rarity and '+' are replaced.
    • Extra stats and runes move with it; a bound item stays bound. It also needs materials.
    • No risk of breaking · no protection stone needed
    • Quality and durability become the average of both items.

    Quality upgrade materials

    • Every quality step needs the item's own tier of Whetstone (Weaponsmith), Reinforced Lining (Armorsmith) and Inlay Powder (Jewelcrafting): 1 each for Rare, 2 for Epic, 3 for Legendary.
    • Epic needs 1 Dungeon Core and Legendary needs 3; cores drop from dungeon bosses.
    • It also costs essence, and the item loses 2 '+' levels. Quality and durability stay the same.
