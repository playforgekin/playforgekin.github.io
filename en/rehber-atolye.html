    Workshop and professions

    The profession chain, crafting step by step, tools and pets.

    Professions

    • Every profession makes another profession's material. None can stand alone.
    • GATHERERS (mining, fishing, herbalism) pull raw goods.
    • PROCESSORS (smelting, alchemy, cooking) turn raw goods into materials.
    • PRODUCERS (weaponsmith, armorsmith, jeweler) make QUALITY UPGRADE materials.
    • The blacksmith, also a producer, makes dungeon keys and repair papers.
    • WEARABLE GEAR CANNOT BE CRAFTED — it drops from monsters. Professions UPGRADE it.
    • ACCESSORIES (rings, earrings, necklace, belt) drop only in DUNGEONS.
    • Buy what you lack on the MARKET — nobody has to level every profession.
    • Tap an item: you see how it is made and WHERE IT IS USED.

    Workshop

    Step by step

    1. Open WORKSHOPS in the Square (or a Hometown building) and pick a profession under SELECT PROFESSION.
    2. Pick a recipe. The recipe shows its cost, its time, what it requires, what it makes, who supplies its materials and who uses the product.
    3. Choose the amount and tap CRAFT. Materials are spent at once; the product arrives when the queue finishes.
    4. The queue keeps running while the game is closed. When you return, finished items are waiting on the queue row.
    5. Tap the green COLLECT button: the items go to your bag and the profession gains EXP. If your bag is full, they stay safely on the crafting page.
    6. Before anything is ready, the same button reads CANCEL: materials for the items not yet made come back to you.
    7. There are two lanes: PRODUCTION for most recipes and MAINTENANCE for repair papers and upgrade stones. Each lane has its own slots.
    8. Missing a material? Tap it and use SHOW RECIPE to see how it is made. If it has no recipe, it comes from gathering, combat or the market.
    9. CRAFT WITH WHAT YOU HAVE lists only what you can make right now from your bag.
    10. Gathering professions work here too: Dynamite digs ore in Mining, Bait catches fish in Fishing, Seed grows herbs in Herbalism. In Adventure your hero also gathers on their own.
    11. Equip a tool in the TOOL CABINET to make its profession faster. A tool only works if your profession level is high enough.

    Craft With What You Have

    • Lists what you can craft right now from your bag and your profession levels.
    • Pick products and amounts, then tap CRAFT.
    • Materials are spent NOW; the product arrives when the queue finishes.
    • Repair papers and upgrade stones use the maintenance slots; other products use the crafting queue.
    • Tap an icon to see the item; SHOW RECIPE opens its recipe.

    Tool Bag

    • Tools for gathering professions: pickaxe, rod, sickle.
    • Tool tier sets your gathering speed and yield.
    • Tools require a PROFESSION level — not a character level.

    Tool Cabinet

    • One tool per profession. An equipped tool makes that profession craft faster.
    • Shortens the time of its own profession only. Can be sold on the market, traded and auctioned. Quality can be upgraded; no rerolls or runes.
    • A tool's rarity and '+' level raise its speed. It needs its profession at the required level; its details show which.
    • Craft time has a lower limit, so tools cannot make crafting instant.

    The profession chain

    • Mining digs ore, Fishing catches fish, Herbalism grows herbs.
    • Smelting turns ore into ingots, Alchemy turns herbs into softening agents, Cooking turns fish into master meals.
    • Ingot + softening agent + master meal of the same tier make the producers' goods: whetstones (Weaponsmith), linings (Armorsmith), inlay powders (Jewelcrafting), keys and repair papers (Blacksmithing).
    • Wearable gear cannot be crafted: it drops from creatures. Professions make it stronger.

    Gathering

    • Gathering professions have no building in Hometown. Your hero gathers in the GATHERING window in ADVENTURE (from Hometown: EXPEDITION GATE).
    • In the workshop you can also spend Dynamite, Bait or Seed to dig, catch or grow.
