    Trade with a player

    What it is

    Swap items and gold directly with another online player, with no tax.

    How to get there

    1. Square: tap a player, then TRADE.
    2. Or a player card: OFFER TRADE.
    3. Or your friends list: INVITE.

    How to do it

    1. Both players must be online. Accept the TRADE INVITE.
    2. Tap items in your bag to put them on the table; add gold if you like.
    3. Lock your offer, then both players tap CONFIRM. Any change resets both confirmations.

    Watch out

    • Bound items, starter items, equipped items and items listed on the market cannot be traded.
    • CANCEL TRADE sends everything back to its owner. Closing the game cancels the trade.
    • There is a daily limit on how much you can trade.
