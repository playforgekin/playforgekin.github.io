    Battle modes

    The battle camp: arena, tower and the boss of the day.

    Battle CampArenaEndless TowerBoss of the Day

    Battle Camp

    • The battle camp: Arena, Tower, Boss of the Day, dungeons and the Ranking are reached from here.
    • A place opens at its level. Tap a sealed place to see when it opens.
    • Stats in the BATTLE PAGES section of the Stats screen work in these fights.

    Arena

    • Winning gives arena rating and ARENA TOKENS; losing still gives a few.
    • Daily rewarded matches are limited; after that you can fight but earn nothing.
    • Opponent power is computed from their gear — '+' level and quality included.
    • Spend tokens in the arena shop on chests and protection scrolls.

    Endless Tower

    • A solo climb, floor by floor. Each floor is stronger than the last.
    • Your highest cleared floor gives a PERMANENT bonus — but only inside the tower.
    • Each floor gives gold and TOWER TOKENS; spend tokens in the tower shop.
    • Tokens are a currency — they take no bag space.
    • The FLOORS page shows upcoming floor strength and rewards in advance.

    Boss of the Day

    • Each day a different boss waits. Set up your team from the FORMATION screen first.
    • Summons are unlimited: trying again costs you nothing.
    • The reward can be claimed once a day. Entry is unlimited — you can help your friends.
    • A win gives a loot box and experience. After a loss you can take a consolation box, but it uses up today's claim.
    • Rewards go to your REWARDS pool.
