    Tier transfer

    What it is

    Move a strong Legendary item up to the next tier without starting over: its '+' level, bonus stats and runes go into the same item one tier higher.

    How to get there

    1. INVENTORY.
    2. Tap the item.
    3. ••• menu: Transfer to T(next tier).

    What you need

    • A Legendary item with a high '+' level. If the item is not ready yet, the transfer screen tells you exactly what is missing.
    • A SHELL: the same item one tier higher, of any rarity, in your bag.
    • Materials of the target tier: Whetstone, Reinforced Lining, Inlay Powder, a Dungeon Core and Legendary Essence. At high '+' levels also upgrade stones.

    How to do it

    1. Open the item and choose the transfer in the ••• menu. Your item is the OLD ITEM.
    2. Tap SHELL and choose the higher tier copy.
    3. Check the RESULT, tap TRANSFER and confirm with Yes, transfer.

    What you get

    • The shell becomes Legendary and takes the old item's bonus stats and runes. Its '+' level drops a little; RESULT shows the new level.
    • Quality and durability become the average of both items.
    • If the old item was equipped, the new one goes into the same slot. A bound item stays bound.

    Watch out

    • No risk of breaking, no protection stone needed.
    • The old item is used up. This cannot be undone.
    • The item must be in your bag or equipped, not in the bank. Charms cannot be transferred.
    • Gathering tools and workshop tools can be transferred too, with the same rules.
