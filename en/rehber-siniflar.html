    How battles work

    • ORDER: your team first, then the enemy — each side in FORMATION ORDER.
    • Weapon speed does NOT change the order; formation does.
    • Everyone gains energy each turn. A skill fires only when its energy and cooldown are ready.
    • ALLIES (everyone but you) are played by the game: the highest-priority ready skill.
    • A ready ultimate goes first; the priest heals the ally with the lowest health.
    • Rows give no damage bonus: the FRONT ROW only shields the BACK ROW from melee.
    • Melee fighters (warrior, priest) can only hit the FRONT row. Ranged can hit anyone.
    • Range comes from the CLASS: archers and mages are ranged, warriors and priests fight in melee.
    • While an enemy is taunting, only it can be hit — the yellow ring marks it.
    • A red ring picks an enemy, a GREEN ring picks the ally you heal. The second tap acts.
    • Healing is calculated from DAMAGE TAKEN: if you were never hit, there is nothing to heal.

    Rows and range

    • Warrior and Priest fight up close and can only hit the FRONT row. Archer and Mage are ranged and can hit anyone.
    • Armor and jewelry can be worn by every class; weapons and off-hands are class items.
    • The Warrior's War Cry taunts: while an enemy is taunting, only it can be hit.
    • In team fights the priest heals the ally with the lowest health.

    Changing class

    • Use CHANGE CLASS in the character menu. It is free up to level 45, after that it costs 200 diamonds.
    • Changing class gives back your skill points, and gear the new class cannot use goes to your bag.
