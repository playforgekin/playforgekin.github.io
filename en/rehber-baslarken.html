    Getting started

    Your first hours: adventure, rewards, quests and what opens when.

    First stepsWhat opens whenAdventureRewardsQuest LogCharacterStatsChatLeaderboardPlayer CardWhere to hunt

    First steps

    1. Pick your class with the arrows on CREATE CHARACTER and enter a name. You can change class later.
    2. Your first fight starts on its own. Short cards then show you Adventure; HOW TO PLAY in the settings starts the full tutorial at any time.
    3. In ADVENTURE, pick a creature and queue the fight. THE GAME KEEPS FIGHTING WHILE IT IS CLOSED.
    4. Finished fights and crafts wait in REWARDS. Collect them to move the loot into your bag.
    5. From level 2 your hero can also gather: the GATHERING window is right below the hunting one in Adventure.
    6. Tap a gear slot to wear a better piece. Every icon in the game tells you what it is when you touch it.
    7. Daily quests advance as you play; finished quests are collected from QUEST LOG.

    Adventure

    • Pick monsters and queue attacks; fights resolve over time on their own.
    • Progress continues while the game is CLOSED — elapsed time counts when you return.
    • The queue and reward pool are limited; when full you cannot queue more.
    • Long press a monster to see its drops and fight duration.

    Rewards

    • Rewards from battles, crafts and quests wait here first.
    • Tap CLAIM ALL to move them into your bag.
    • When the pool is full you cannot start NEW ATTACKS — collect first.
    • If your bag is full the package stays in the pool — nothing is lost.
    • Profession rewards add XP to THAT profession, not your character level.

    Quest Log

    • Daily quests renew every day; weekly quests are on the WEEKLY tab. Quests advance as you play.
    • Tap GO to jump to where a quest is done. When it is finished, tap COLLECT.
    • Quest rewards go to your REWARDS pool. Open REWARDS to move them into your bag.
    • Claim enough quests to open the QUEST CHEST for an extra reward.

    Character

    • Manage your gear, stats and appearance here.
    • Double tap to equip; long press opens the info panel.
    • Broken (fully worn) gear gives NO stats. A REPAIR PAPER brings it back.

    Stats

    • All your stats from gear in one place: quality, '+' level and jewelry set bonuses add up here.
    • Tap a stat to see what it does.
    • Stats in the BATTLE PAGES section work only on the Battle page, not in Adventure or Dungeons.

    Chat

    • There are GLOBAL, GUILD and WHISPER tabs.
    • Messages are sent with the SEND button — Enter does not send.
    • Type '@' and pick an item to link it in chat; others can tap to view it.

    Leaderboard

    • Tap a player and choose INSPECT to see their gear and stats.
    • Tap the character image to enlarge it full screen.

    Player Card

    • This player's gear, '+' levels, quality and profession levels.
    • Tap a piece of gear to see what it is and its quality tier.
    • Tap the image to open it full screen.
    • From here you can add a friend, offer a trade, or browse their listings.

    Where to hunt

    • A new hunting ground opens every few levels; each one is harder and pays better. Climb only as fast as your gear allows.
    • Dungeons open between the hunting grounds and each needs a key of its tier.
    • Whispering Forest Lv 1
    • Foggy Swamp Lv 11
    • Cursed Graveyard Lv 21
    • Frozen Mountains Lv 31
    • Golden Desert Lv 41
    • Crystal Caves Lv 51
    • Volcano Peak Lv 61
    • Abyss Void Lv 71
    • Holy Sanctuary Lv 81
    • Dragon Peaks Lv 91
