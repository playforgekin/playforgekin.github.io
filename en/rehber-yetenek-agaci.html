    How the tree works

    • Each class has 10 rows with 2 skills each. Each skill goes up to rank 5.
    • You earn skill points as you level up; the tree shows how many you have.
    • Each row opens at a character level; a locked row shows the level it unlocks at.
    • You can never earn enough points for the whole tree: choose your build.
    • Active skills evolve: Normal, then Rare, then Epic. An evolved form opens only when the one below it is at 5/5.
    • Resetting gives back every spent point and your first skill stays open. Its list price is 100 diamonds, but it is free for now.
