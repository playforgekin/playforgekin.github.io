    How the tree works

    • Each class has 10 rows with 2 skills each. Each skill goes up to rank 5.
    • From level 2 to level 50 you get 1 skill point at every level; after that 1 point every 2.5 levels. You also get 1 free point for your first skill.
    • Skill rows open at levels 1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 35, 45, 60, 75 and 90. To use a row, spend at least 1 point in the row below it.
    • Skills from level 60 cost 2 points per rank. You can earn at most 70 points, far less than the whole tree needs: choose your build.
    • Active skills evolve: Normal, then Rare, then Epic. An evolved form opens only when the one below it is at 5/5.
    • Resetting gives back every spent point and your first skill stays open. Its list price is 100 diamonds, but it is free for now.
