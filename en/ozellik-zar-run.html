    Reroll and runes

    What it is

    Reroll gives an item a new Quality and new bonus stats; a rune lets you choose a stat yourself. Both spend essence of the item's rarity and gold.

    How to get there

    1. INVENTORY.
    2. Tap the item.
    3. ••• menu: Reroll / Rune.

    Reroll

    • REROLL rolls the item's Quality and every unlocked bonus stat line again. The result can be better or worse, and it cannot be undone.
    • Higher rarity items have more bonus stat lines. An EMPTY STAT LINE is filled by the next reroll.
    • POSSIBLE REROLL STATS lists every stat a reroll can give.
    • Imprinted runes are never touched by a reroll.

    Locks

    • Before rerolling you can lock the bonus stat lines you want to keep, and lock the Quality on its own. Locks are paid with diamonds; each lock shows its price.
    • At least one line must still be rerolled. Normal items cannot lock lines.
    • The REROLL button tells you how many lines will be rolled and what is locked.

    Runes

    • Rune slots come with rarity: Normal items have none, and every rarity step opens one more.
    • Tap EMPTY RUNE SLOT, pick a stat that is not on the item yet and tap IMPRINT RUNE. The value is rolled within the bonus stat range.
    • A stat can be on an item only once: stats the item already has are greyed out.
    • Tap a filled slot to replace its rune. The old rune is removed and the full cost is paid again. A rune cannot be removed without putting a new one in.
    • Runes stay on the item through rerolls, quality upgrades and tier transfers.

    What you need

    • Essence of the item's rarity (Normal, Rare, Epic or Legendary Essence) and gold. Higher tier items need more. A rune costs more than a reroll.
    • Locks cost diamonds. The COST row shows what you need and what you have before you tap.

    Watch out

    • Works on equipped items too. An item listed on the market must be taken off the market first.
    • Workshop tools have no rerolls and no runes.
    • Short on essence? Dismantle gear you do not need.
