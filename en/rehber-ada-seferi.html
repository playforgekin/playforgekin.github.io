    Ship and islands

    Build the ship, sail to the islands, collect and repair.

    ShipyardIslandsBuilding and repairing the shipIsland list

    Shipyard

    • Build your ship stage by stage. Donate gold and crafted materials; you can give a little at a time.
    • Pick up to the allowed number of islands. The ship arrives at once.
    • At each island: PLAY it yourself (loot right away) or send it on AUTO (loot when the ship is back).
    • Auto unlocks for an island after you clear it yourself once.
    • Each island's chest often holds the parts of its own pet.
    • After the last stop the ship sails home. It comes back damaged: repair it to sail again.

    Step by step

    1. Open HOMETOWN and tap the SHIPYARD sign.
    2. Build the ship stage by stage: tap GIVE on a gold or material cell, or DELIVER ALL I CAN. Donations cannot be taken back.
    3. When the ship is ready, open VOYAGE, tap islands to add them to the route, then SET SAIL. The ship arrives at once.
    4. At each island choose PLAY (play it yourself, the chest opens right away) or AUTO (loot comes when the ship is back). Auto unlocks after you clear the island yourself once.
    5. After the last stop the ship sails home. When it is back in port, tap COLLECT ALL; the voyage report lists every stop's loot.
    6. The ship comes home damaged. Tap REPAIR and deliver the repair materials the same way you built it.

    Islands

    • Islands are reached by ship. The ship is built and repaired at the Shipyard.
    • At each island, play it yourself or send it on auto. Auto opens for an island after you clear it yourself once.
    • Each island has its own chest and its own Pet Box; that island's pet shards come up more often.

    Building and repairing the ship

    • The ship is built in stages with Normal Essence and gold; the contribute panel shows what is given and what is still needed.
    • Pick up to 3 islands for a voyage. After the last stop the ship needs some hours to come back; the screen shows how long.
    • Every return damages the hull, so the ship must be repaired before it sails again.
