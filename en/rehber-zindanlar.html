    Dungeons

    Keys, rows of creatures and the dungeon boss.

    Dungeon

    • Dungeon entry costs a KEY; blacksmiths craft keys.
    • You pick monsters row by row; each row opens after the one before it.
    • Your time budget is limited: the total time of your picks cannot exceed it.
    • GEAR FROM DUNGEONS DROPS AS RARE — map monsters drop Normal.
    • The dungeon queue is SEPARATE from the world queue and ignores the attack limit.

    Step by step

    1. Open a dungeon: its key is spent when you enter.
    2. Pick one creature in each ROW, then tap START DUNGEON. Rows left empty cannot be picked again in that run.
    3. The fights then run in the queue. LEAVE DUNGEON does not refund the key.
