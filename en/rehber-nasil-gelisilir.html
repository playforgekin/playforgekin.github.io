    How to progress

    New to Forgekin? Follow these steps from your first fight to your first Legendary item.

    1. Create your hero (level 1)

    1. Use the arrows to choose your class: Warrior, Archer, Mage or Priest. Type a name and tap CREATE.
    2. Your first fight starts on its own, on the first floor of the Endless Tower. Win it and your first skill is yours.
    3. Not happy with your class? You can change it later in the settings (CHANGE CLASS).

    Where to go: CREATE CHARACTER screen, then the Tower.

    Read more: How a fight looks · Endless Tower

    2. Hunt in Adventure

    1. Open Adventure. Choose the creatures (In turn, or tap one) and tap START: your hero fills the queue and keeps hunting, EVEN WHILE THE GAME IS CLOSED.
    2. When fights are done, tap COLLECT. CLAIM ALL at the bottom collects every window at once. Loot goes to your bag, EXP to your level.
    3. When the rewards pool is full you cannot queue new fights: collect first.
    4. The goal strip at the top of Adventure always shows your next goal.
    5. Before you close the game, fill the queue: your hero works while you are away.

    Where to go: Bottom bar, second tab: ADVENTURE. Once your Hometown opens the tab is called HOMETOWN: tap the EXPEDITION GATE.

    Read more: Adventure · Collecting rewards · Zones and maps

    3. Gather from level 2

    1. At level 2 the GATHERING window opens in Adventure. Pick Mining, Fishing or Herbalism and tap START: your hero mines, fishes and picks herbs on their own.
    2. Gathering raises that profession's level, not your character level.
    3. A gathering tool (pickaxe, rod or sickle) makes it faster. Tools drop from creatures.

    Where to go: Adventure: the GATHERING window.

    Read more: Gathering · Tools

    4. Wear better gear

    1. Gear drops from creatures. In INVENTORY, EQUIP BEST puts on your strongest pieces in one tap.
    2. Tap any icon to see what it is. The ••• menu of an item holds upgrade, sell and dismantle.
    3. Wearable gear is not crafted: it drops from creatures and dungeons, or you buy it on the market.
    4. The HELP CHEST in the Square gives free gear to new players.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: INVENTORY (far left).

    Screen shown in Turkish.

    Read more: Inventory and character · Item info · Rarity · Help Chest

    5. Daily quests and the Square

    1. The game opens in the SQUARE. Its signs lead to the Notice Board, Market, Guild, Help Chest and Workshops.
    2. NOTICE BOARD, DAILY QUESTS: quests advance as you play. GO takes you to the right screen, COLLECT takes the reward. There is a WEEKLY tab too.
    3. LOGIN CALENDAR gives a reward every day you play.
    4. Lost? HOW TO PLAY in the settings starts the tutorial again, and most screens have a (?) that explains them.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: SQUARE (middle).

    Read more: The Square · Daily quests · Login calendar · Tutorial and how to play

    6. Spend your skill points

    1. You earn skill points as you level up. A badge on SKILLS shows that you have points to spend.
    2. Each class has its own tree. Rows open as you level up; points open and rank up the skills in them. A maxed active skill can evolve.
    3. You cannot open everything, so choose a build. RESET SKILLS gives your points back.

    Where to go: INVENTORY, then SKILLS (top left of your character).

    Read more: Skill tree

    7. Professions: gather, process, craft

    1. Every profession makes another profession's material. Gatherers (Mining, Fishing, Herbalism) bring raw materials, processors (Smelting, Alchemy, Cooking) refine them, crafters (Blacksmithing, Armorsmith, Jewelcrafting, Weaponsmith) finish them.
    2. Open WORKSHOPS, SELECT PROFESSION, pick a recipe and tap CRAFT. The queue keeps running while the game is closed; COLLECT the products later.
    3. Missing a material? Tap it and use SHOW RECIPE. CRAFT WITH WHAT YOU HAVE lists what you can make right now.
    4. Professions make dungeon keys, upgrade stones, repair papers and the materials that raise your gear's rarity.
    5. In your Hometown, ARTISANS' STREET has a master for each profession: tap one to open their workshop.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: SQUARE, then the WORKSHOPS sign.

    Screen shown in Turkish.

    Read more: Workshop · Craft with what you have · Tools

    8. From level 5: dungeons, market, arena

    1. Dungeons drop accessories, Rare gear and dungeon cores. Each dungeon needs a key of its own tier.
    2. Keys are crafted by Blacksmithing from an ingot, a softening agent and a master meal, or bought on the market.
    3. Enter from Adventure: SOLO DUNGEON at the top. Play with others in GROUP DUNGEON.
    4. The MARKET opens: sell what you do not need and buy what you are missing.
    5. The ARENA opens in BATTLE: fight other players' heroes for arena tokens.

    Where to go: Adventure: SOLO DUNGEON. Square: MARKET. Bottom bar: BATTLE.

    Screen shown in Turkish.

    Read more: Solo dungeon · Party dungeon · Market · Arena

    9. Make your gear stronger

    1. '+' upgrade: upgrade stones raise the '+' level. At high levels a failure can break the item: use a protection stone.
    2. Upgrade Quality: raise the rarity step by step, Normal to Rare to Epic to Legendary.
    3. Reroll and runes: roll new bonus stats or imprint the stat you want.
    4. Dismantle gear you do not need: it gives upgrade stones and essence for all of the above.
    5. Tier transfer: move a strong Legendary item to the next tier without losing its work.
    6. Gear wears in fights. A broken item gives no stats: repair it with a repair paper.
    7. Set bonus: when all your gear, or all your jewelry, is Legendary, you get extra attack and health. Complete both for more EXP and gold.

    Where to go: INVENTORY: tap an item, then UPGRADE or the ••• menu.

    10. Battle modes

    1. The BATTLE tab is a camp with a sign for each mode: Endless Tower, Arena, Boss of the Day, Ranking, Expeditions and Survival. A locked sign shows the level it opens at.
    2. The Tower and the Arena give tokens; spend them in their shops in the STORE.
    3. Expedition and Survival bosses can drop workshop tools.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: BATTLE (fourth tab).

    Screen shown in Turkish.

    Read more: The Battle screen · Endless Tower · Arena · Boss of the Day · Expeditions · Survival

    11. From level 10: join a guild

    1. Join a guild or found your own. Donate to level it up, fight the guild boss together and shop in the guild shop.
    2. During a season, kingdoms fight kingdom wars.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: SQUARE, then the GUILD sign.

    Screen shown in Turkish.

    Read more: Guild · Guild boss · Kingdoms

    12. Hometown, ship and pets

    1. Your HOMETOWN: upgrade the PROCESSING and CRAFTING HALL buildings with gold and materials.
    2. From level 20 the SHIPYARD opens: build a ship and sail to the islands. Island chests hold pet boxes.
    3. Pets: collect shards to unlock a pet. A companion pet speeds up its own profession; pets do not fight.

    Where to go: Bottom bar: HOMETOWN. Pets: INVENTORY, then PET.

    Read more: Hometown · Shipyard and voyages · Pets

    Good habits

    • Keep the Adventure queue and the workshop queue full before you close the game.
    • Collect rewards often: a full pool stops new fights.
    • Climb to a new hunting ground only as fast as your gear allows.
    • Repair before your gear breaks, and keep a repair paper in your bag.
    • Every icon in the game opens its info: tap anything you do not know.
