    How to get essence

    • Dismantle (salvage) gear you do not need: you get essence of the item's own quality. Normal gear gives Normal Essence, Rare gear gives Rare Essence, and so on.
    • Higher tier items give more essence. The dismantle window always shows exactly what you will get.
    • Some essence also comes from daily quests, mini game tokens and the guild shop: see each essence below.

    Combining essence

    • Tap an essence in your bag and press MERGE: lower essence turns into the next quality.
    • Merge ratio: x5 → x1

    What essence is for

    Quality upgrade, stat reroll and runes all spend essence of the item's quality.
