    Upgrade (+)

    What it is

    Raise an item's '+' level with upgrade stones. Every '+' makes its stats stronger, up to +15.

    How to get there

    1. INVENTORY.
    2. Tap the item.
    3. UPGRADE.

    How to do it

    1. Tap UPGRADE in the item info. NOW and AFTER show what changes, with the chance of success and the stones that will be used.
    2. Tap UPGRADE +n. Equipped items are upgraded from their own info panel.
    3. At +15 the item shows MAX and cannot go higher.

    What you need

    • Upgrade Stones of the item's tier. Charms also cost gold.
    • Stones come from dismantling gear, from Smelting in the workshop and from the market.

    Failing and breaking

    • A failed attempt uses the stones, but the item keeps its '+'. Every failure raises the chance of the next try at the same step.
    • At high '+' levels the screen shows BREAK RISK: a failed attempt there DESTROYS the item. Without protection the game asks you twice.

    Protection stone

    • An Upgrade Protection Stone stops the item from breaking. At lower levels it raises the chance of success instead.
    • One is used on every attempt, successful or not. At risky levels it is switched on for you if you have one; you can turn it OFF.
