    Invasion

    • Everyone fights the same invading boss together. Every attack wears down its health.
    • Attacks are limited each day; the screen shows how many you have left.
    • Bought at the invasion entrance. Unused ones are lost when the battle ends.
    • Players are ranked by damage. The top players earn special rewards, and everyone who joins gets a reward too.
    • Rewards arrive by mail. Hidden rewards are revealed when the invasion ends.
