    Workshop

    What it is

    Pick a profession, choose a recipe and add it to the queue. Each profession makes another profession's material.

    How to get there

    1. Bottom bar: SQUARE.
    2. Tap the WORKSHOPS sign.
    3. Or tap an item, then SHOW RECIPE.
