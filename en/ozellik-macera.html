    Adventure

    What it is

    Queue fights against creatures and let them run, even while you are away. Pick a zone with CHANGE, mark creatures, START, then collect REWARDS.

    How to get there

    1. Bottom bar: HOMETOWN (second tab).
    2. Tap the EXPEDITION GATE.
