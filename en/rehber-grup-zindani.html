    Party dungeon

    Run a dungeon together: party, keys, shared damage and loot.

    Party Dungeon

    • Run the same dungeon together with other players.
    • Everyone's damage goes into a shared pool; the last hit does not matter.
    • Rewards follow contribution — idling along earns nothing.
    • The leader picks the zone; anyone not ready holds up the start.

    Step by step

    1. On the dungeon screen open PARTY DUNGEON and pick a dungeon that fits your level. You must be online.
    2. JOIN an open party or CREATE PARTY. The leader can INVITE A FRIEND and KICK members.
    3. Every member taps READY: your keys are held and come back if the party breaks up. More different classes make the run faster.
    4. The leader taps START DUNGEON when everyone is ready. Keys are spent from every member at that moment.
    5. Tap a monster card to pour your remaining time into it. Damage is shared, and every kill pays every member with their own roll.
    6. END RUN finishes early. Rewards go to your reward pool; spent keys are not refunded if you leave.
