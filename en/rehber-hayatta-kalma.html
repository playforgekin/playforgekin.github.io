    Survival

    Survive the horde, pick cards, beat the bosses.

    Survival

    • Enter alone and survive the horde. Your normal skills stay outside; inside you build up weapons and passives.
    • Collect crystals to level up; each level lets you pick a card.
    • Tap a card to see it, tap again to take it.
    • A weapon at its top level plus its matching passive evolves into a stronger weapon.
    • A boss arrives from time to time. Rewards come at wave and boss milestones: the further you get, the more you earn.
    • Play as many runs as you like; only the first runs of each day give rewards. Your best wave of the week counts on the SURVIVAL RANKING.

    Step by step

    1. Open the Battle camp and tap the SURVIVAL gate. The box shows your best run and the rewards by wave and by boss.
    2. Tap ENTER. Move with the joystick; your hero attacks on their own.
    3. Pick up crystals. On each level up the run pauses and you choose a card: a weapon, a passive, an upgrade, or skip it for health.
    4. MAGNET pulls nearby crystals to you. A strip warns you before each boss.
    5. PAUSE saves the run: SAVE & EXIT and continue later, or END RUN. Leaving the app also pauses it.
    6. There is no time limit: the run ends when you fall or leave. Rewards for the waves and bosses you reached go to your reward pool.

    Rewards

    • Survival rewards Normal Essence for each boss you defeat: the more bosses, the more essence. The REWARDS preview shows what each boss gives you.
    • Leaving early keeps the rewards for what you reached.
    • There is no limit on runs or run time. Only the first runs of each day give rewards; after that you can keep playing for the ranking.
    • SURVIVAL RANKING lists the highest wave of the week. On a tie, the faster run wins.
    • After you play once yourself, SEND starts an auto run that gives a smaller reward.
