    Kingdoms

    Choosing a kingdom and fighting kingdom wars.

    Kingdom

      KINGDOMS

      • Pick one of the two kingdoms. You can switch freely until the seal; after the seal your kingdom is final.
      • SEAL MY CHOICE makes your choice final right away. Sealing can never be undone.
      • Talk with your guild before you choose: kingdom wars are fought together.
      • Kingdom wars open after the seal. Use GO TO KINGDOM WARS to join them.

      Kingdom War

      • When the banners rise, join a group or start your own. The group leader accepts players; players left without a group may be placed in one by the kingdom.
      • Only the leader starts an attack, and every member must be present and READY.
      • An attack is spent when the fight starts. Leaving the fight does not give it back.
      • Save your defense: your gear and stats are copied, and enemy groups fight that copy.
      • Only won attacks give points; losing costs nothing. A HARD opponent gives bonus points.
      • Use all of a day's attacks and you get a reward by mail for each fight; won fights give more. When the war ends, the kingdom with more points wins.
