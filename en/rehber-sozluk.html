    Glossary

    Short explanations of the game's words.

    Terms

    • Bound: cannot be traded or sold. You can still use it or salvage it.
    • Durability: gear wears as you fight. A broken item gives no stats; a repair paper brings them all back.
    • Quality: each item rolls its own quality percentage; higher quality means slightly higher stats. A reroll draws it again.
    • Rarity: Normal, Rare, Epic, Legendary. Higher rarity gives better stats and more rune slots.
    • Tier (T1-T10): the material level of an item. Names tell the tier: Copper is T1, Flamerite is T10.
    • '+' level: raised with upgrade stones. At high '+' levels a failed attempt can break the item unless you use a protection stone.
    • Salvage: breaking an item gives essence of its rarity. Essence pays for rerolls and runes.
    • Rune: you pick the stat, its value is fixed and it survives rerolls.
    • Reroll: quality and all bonus stats are rolled again; cheap, but the result may be worse.
    • Reward pool: rewards wait here until you collect them, for example when your bag is full.
    • Held items: things you own that are not in your bag right now (party keys, rewards, boxes) and how each one comes back.
    • Memory Boxes: a team event; each box hides a picture and you open it for your team by winning a fight.
    • Time Warp: finishes a running timer right away.
    • Premium: more bag slots, a bigger queue and reward pool. It gives no combat power.
